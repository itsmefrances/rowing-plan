Programming overview — for review

13-Week Erg + Strength Block

7 Sep – 6 Dec 2026

Three fixed Rowfit erg sessions each week — sprint, VO₂, threshold — with strength programmed around them. The strength block targets the 500 m and the 1-minute; the 2000 m on 6 Dec is an endpoint, not the primary target. Warm-ups, cool-downs and tier-3 accessory work are omitted here.

The training week

The repeating shape — test weeks, races and the taper vary; see week by week

The three erg sessions are fixed points — they are never moved or dropped to accommodate a lift. Strength sits on the days between them, so a hard erg session is never run on the back of a heavy leg day. Sunday is full rest.
