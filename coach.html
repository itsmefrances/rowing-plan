Programming overview — for review

13-Week Erg + Strength Block

7 Sep – 6 Dec 2026

Three fixed Rowfit erg sessions each week — sprint, VO₂, threshold — with strength programmed around them. The strength block targets the 500 m and the 1-minute; the 2000 m on 6 Dec is an endpoint, not the primary target. Warm-ups, cool-downs and tier-3 accessory work are omitted here.

The three erg sessions are fixed points — they are never moved or dropped to accommodate a lift. Strength sits on the days between them, so a hard erg session is never run on the back of a heavy leg day. Sunday is full rest.

Measurement

Countermovement jump, read live from the training log

Three jumps, 30 s apart, hands on hips, before anything else touches her legs, with a few low-effort jumps first so the test reads readiness and not warm-up state. The mean of the three is scored, not the best — averaged jump height tracks neuromuscular status more sensitively than peak, and her best attempt has been drifting later in the set, which is exactly the noise a mean sees through. Against a baseline set at the mean of her three best sessions, the session scores green at or above −5%, amber to −10%, red below. On an intensive day amber removes one set from each jump and leaves the lifting alone; red removes the max-intent jumping altogether.
